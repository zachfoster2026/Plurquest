PLURQuest / Who We Are

Built by people who wanted something like this to exist.

PLURQuest is a small event-activation project rooted in festival culture, making interactive things because the best part of an event is often the unexpected moment between the scheduled ones.

Our approach

Handmade on purpose.

Our work is designed, built, repaired, transported, stocked and operated by real humans. The rough edges, handwritten rules, changing decorations and community contributions are part of the identity—not flaws to be sanded away.

Make the thing people wander into, then talk about on the walk back to camp.

P

Peace

Build spaces that lower the temperature and invite people in.

L

Love

Gifting and generosity are part of the mechanic, not a marketing layer.

U/R

Unity & respect

The experience works when people feel comfortable participating together.
