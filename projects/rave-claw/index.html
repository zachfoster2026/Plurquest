Rave Claw Machine

Free to play. One prize per person. A ridiculous little idea that kept growing.

How it feels

Arcade game meets gifting culture.

Rave Claw takes a familiar claw machine and removes the transaction. The play is free, the prizes are community-minded, and the machine becomes a little gathering point wherever it lands.

The evolution

It did not stay small.

THE START

One small machine

The concept began simply: put a claw machine into a community setting and let people play for free.

ROAMING

Then the machine started moving

Portable versions turned the activation into something that could travel through the crowd and become part of quests, trades and chance encounters.

FOREST

The builds became part of the environment

Cabin styling, moss, wood textures and hidden placements helped the machines feel discovered rather than installed.

SCALE

Full-size activations followed

Multiple stationary machines, larger prize capacity and event-scale operations pushed the project beyond its tiny-machine roots.
